Gobble Dash — The Great Turkey Escape. A side-scrolling runner: a turkey sprints 1200 m from the farmyard, through the barn, into the kitchen and out to the forest, chased the whole way by the farmer. Jump the fences, hay bales, crates, mud puddles and the glowing kitchen oven; duck the farmer's thrown carving knives and the knives falling from above. Grab corn to push the farmer back, feathers and golden corn for bonus points. Reach the forest gate and the turkey lives to gobble another day; let the farmer catch up and it is dinner time.

Title screen, HUD (score, distance to the forest, farmer gap meter, stage, best score), pause, and caught/escaped end screens with restart. Keyboard and mouse: Space / Up / W or click to jump, Down / S to duck and slide, P / Esc to pause, R to restart, M to mute. All art is drawn and all sounds are synthesised in code — no assets, no network requests, nothing placeholder.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — or just open `game/index.html` in a browser. No build step, no network requests, no account.
